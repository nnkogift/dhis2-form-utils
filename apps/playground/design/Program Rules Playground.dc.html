What breaks down today: a tracker program renders only its registration form — the program's stages and events are unreachable, so stage rules can never fire. The org unit and date fields sit in the form flow as if they were captured data. Rule state is split across two rails that don't talk to the form: nothing on a field tells you a rule touched it, and a hidden field simply vanishes.
